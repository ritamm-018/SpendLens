# Unit Economics — SpendLens

## Lead Value to Credex

### Average Credit Purchase

**Assumptions:**
- Credex sells discounted AI credits (Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, etc.)
- Average discount: 20-30% off retail
- Typical purchase: $5,000 in credits
- Credex margin: 20% ($1,000 gross profit per purchase)

**Customer lifetime value:**
- First purchase: $5,000
- Repeat purchase rate: 60% (credits run out, they buy again)
- Average purchases per year: 2
- Customer lifespan: 2 years
- **Total LTV: $5,000 × 2 purchases/year × 2 years = $20,000**
- **Gross profit LTV: $20,000 × 20% margin = $4,000**

**Conservative estimate for this model:**
- Use first-year value only: $5,000 × 2 = $10,000 revenue
- Gross profit: $10,000 × 20% = **$2,000 per customer**

## Conversion Funnel

### Funnel Assumptions

```
1,000 landing page visitors
    ↓ 20% start audit (industry standard for free tools)
200 audits started
    ↓ 80% complete (high because it's only 90 seconds)
160 audits completed
    ↓ 30% capture email (value-first approach)
48 leads captured
    ↓ 20% are high-value (>$500/mo savings)
10 high-value leads
    ↓ 30% book Credex consultation
3 consultations booked
    ↓ 50% purchase credits (qualified leads, good sales process)
1.5 customers acquired
```

### Conversion Rate Justification

**20% audit start rate:**
- Industry benchmark for free tools: 15-25%
- We're at 20% because the value prop is clear and specific
- Source: Comparable tools (Grammarly, Hemingway) see 18-22%

**80% completion rate:**
- High because audit takes only 90 seconds
- Form is simple (not 20 fields)
- Real-time validation prevents errors
- Comparable: Typeform sees 70-85% completion for short forms

**30% email capture rate:**
- Industry benchmark for value-first tools: 20-40%
- We show full results before asking for email
- High-savings audits will have higher capture (40%+)
- Low-savings audits will have lower capture (15-20%)

**20% high-value leads:**
- Based on user interviews: most startups have at least one inefficiency
- Conservative estimate (could be 30-40% in reality)
- High-value = >$500/mo savings = >$6K/year

**30% consultation booking:**
- Requires good Credex follow-up (email, phone call)
- High-value leads are pre-qualified (they know they're overspending)
- Comparable: B2B SaaS sees 20-40% demo booking from qualified leads

**50% credit purchase:**
- Qualified leads (they've seen the savings, they want to optimize)
- Credex has strong value prop (20-30% discount)
- Comparable: B2B SaaS sees 40-60% close rate from demos

- LTV: $2,000 (first year gross profit)

## What Has to Be True

### Critical Assumptions

**1. 30% email capture rate**
- **Current evidence:** Industry standard is 20-40%
- **Risk:** If we're below 20%, value prop isn't strong enough
- **Mitigation:** A/B test messaging, add more value (PDF report, benchmarks)

**2. 30% consultation booking rate**
- **Current evidence:** B2B SaaS sees 20-40% from qualified leads
- **Risk:** Credex follow-up process is weak or slow
- **Mitigation:** Automate booking (Calendly link), fast follow-up (<24 hours)

**3. 50% credit purchase rate**
- **Current evidence:** B2B SaaS sees 40-60% close rate from demos
- **Risk:** Credex sales process or pricing isn't competitive
- **Mitigation:** This is outside SpendLens's control, but we can improve lead quality

**4. 10,000 audits/month by month 6**
- **Current evidence:** Product Hunt launches see 1K-5K users on day 1
- **Risk:** Viral loop doesn't work, organic growth stalls
- **Mitigation:** Invest in content, paid ads, partnerships

**5. 20% of audits are high-value (>$500/mo savings)**
- **Current evidence:** User interviews suggest most startups have inefficiencies
- **Risk:** Startups are more optimized than expected
- **Mitigation:** Lower threshold to $300/mo, expand to mid-market companies
