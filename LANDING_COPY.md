## FAQ

**Q: Is this really free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no signup required. We built this to help startups optimize AI spend and as a lead-gen tool for Credex (our parent company that sells discounted AI credits).

**Q: How accurate are the recommendations?**
A: Very. We use real pricing data verified against official vendor sources. Our audit logic is deterministic and conservative — we'd rather underestimate savings than overestimate. Every recommendation includes a confidence score (high/medium/low) so you know which to trust.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: We store anonymized audit results for public sharing (if you choose to share). Email is optional and only used to send your report. We never sell data or share it with third parties.

**Q: What tools do you analyze?**
A: Currently: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, OpenAI API, Anthropic API, Windsurf, and v0. We're adding more tools based on user requests.

**Q: Can I share my results?**
A: Yes! Every audit gets a unique shareable URL with social preview cards. Great for showing your finance team or comparing with other founders.

**Q: How often should I audit?**
A: We recommend quarterly audits. Pricing changes, team sizes change, and new startup credit programs launch regularly. Set a calendar reminder for every 3 months.

**Q: What if I'm already optimized?**
A: Great! We'll tell you that. The "already optimized" path is just as valuable — it gives you confidence that you're not wasting money. You'll also get benchmark data showing how you compare to similar teams.

**Q: Do you offer consulting?**
A: Not directly, but our parent company Credex offers AI credit purchasing and optimization consulting. If your audit shows high savings potential (>$500/month), we can connect you with their team.

**Q: Can I audit multiple teams?**
A: Yes, just run separate audits for each team. Each audit gets its own unique URL and results.

**Q: What if my tool isn't listed?**
A: Email us at hello@spendlens.com with the tool name and we'll add it. We prioritize tools based on user requests.
