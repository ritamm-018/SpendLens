## Pricing Verification Process

**How we verify pricing:**
1. Visit official vendor pricing page
2. Screenshot pricing table
3. Record verification date
4. Check for promotional pricing or discounts
5. Note any "Contact sales" plans with estimates
6. Re-verify quarterly

**When we estimate:**
- Enterprise plans with no public pricing
- Custom plans that require sales contact
- Volume discounts that vary by customer

**Estimation methodology:**
- Compare to competitor pricing
- Use industry benchmarks (2-3x business plan pricing)
- Conservative estimates (prefer underestimating)

## Notes

**Why some prices are estimates:**
Enterprise pricing is rarely public. Vendors use "Contact sales" to negotiate based on company size, usage, and budget. Our estimates are conservative and based on:
1. Competitor analysis
2. Industry benchmarks
3. Conversations with founders who have enterprise plans

**Pricing volatility:**
AI tool pricing changes frequently (every 3-6 months). We re-verify quarterly and update this document. If you notice outdated pricing, email hello@spendlens.com.

**Regional pricing:**
All prices are USD and based on US pricing. Some vendors offer regional pricing (e.g., PPP discounts for certain countries). Our audit assumes US pricing.

**Volume discounts:**
Many vendors offer volume discounts for 50+ seats or high API usage. These are negotiated case-by-case and not reflected in our standard pricing data.
