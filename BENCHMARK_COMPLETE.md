## 🧠 Intelligence Features

### **Smart Results Generation**
The `generateBenchmarkResults()` function creates personalized results based on:

1. **Efficiency Score Calculation**
   - Analyzes spend-per-person ratio
   - Optimal range: $50-100/person/month
   - Adjusts for use case (API-heavy expected to spend more)
   - Returns score 30-95 (realistic range)

2. **Personalized Insights**
   - Use case-specific recommendations
   - Team size-appropriate advice
   - Efficiency-based suggestions
   - Feels intelligent, not templated

3. **Company Archetype Assignment**
   - Based on spend patterns and efficiency
   - Memorable names and descriptions
   - Creates shareability

4. **Realistic Benchmarks**
   - Industry averages calculated from team size
   - Most efficient teams at 67% of average
   - Percentile ranking with distribution
