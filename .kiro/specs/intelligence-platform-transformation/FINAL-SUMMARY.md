## 🎨 Design System

### Colors & Gradients
- **Green**: `from-emerald-400 via-green-500 to-teal-600` (high scores)
- **Yellow**: `from-amber-400 via-yellow-500 to-orange-500` (medium scores)
- **Red**: `from-orange-400 via-red-500 to-pink-600` (low scores)
- **Blue**: `from-blue-600 via-cyan-600 to-teal-600` (primary brand)
- **Purple**: `from-purple-500 to-pink-600` (accents)

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Font-black (900 weight), tracking-tight
- **Scores**: 9xl size (128px), gradient text
- **Body**: Font-medium/semibold, leading-relaxed
- **Labels**: Font-semibold, smaller sizes

### Spacing
- **Cards**: p-6 to p-12 (generous padding)
- **Gaps**: gap-4 to gap-8 (consistent spacing)
- **Margins**: mb-12 to mb-16 (clear sections)
- **Rounded**: rounded-2xl to rounded-3xl (smooth corners)

### Effects
- **Glassmorphism**: backdrop-blur-xl, bg-white/40
- **Shadows**: shadow-lg to shadow-2xl with color tints
- **Hover**: y: -4px transform, opacity transitions
- **Animations**: Spring physics, staggered delays
